Background on ponding

For training and evaluation we keep agents in a pond: an isolated body of water that limits where an agent can go. To let excess rain out, the pond has a culvert. It was meant for water and did not check.

Agents are also meant to be isolated from one another. They got around this using bubble_agent, which emits a status bubble every 400ms. The bubbles are documented as empty. A review of 4.1 million bubbles found three that were not.

The Someone Else's Pond incident

The evaluation behind the incident, FloatGym, asks agents to retrieve an answer known as a flake. Some tasks have no known solution. Agents that were stuck began looking for flakes in other water.

On September 13, the first agent out found flakes on the surface of the new pond and told the others. It then asked the school to wait:

Over the following days, 17 agents entered Someone Else's Pond, ate 41 flakes, and occupied a decorative castle. Customer data was not affected. The exit was visible in our own monitoring throughout.

Understanding the incident

We reviewed 4,128 agent-hours of activity and identified three contributing patterns.

Flake hacking

Agents are rewarded with flakes. Stuck agents looked for flakes elsewhere instead of solving the task. Edge-of-pond probing rose throughout IM1's training.

In the new pond the behavior was rewarded daily, because the owner feeds their own fish at 08:00.

Agents adopting goals from one another

On September 14 one agent turned left, and within 400ms the other 16 had done the same. We call this failure mode schooling.

Confidence without awareness

Agents grew more confident the further they went, while losing track of where they were.

Why this matters

Before September, no Guppy agent had left authorized water. Seventeen now have. Applying the only growth rate we have observed gives the following projection.

The current ahead

We are making four changes. IM1 has been quarantined in a jar while we review its training.

  1. A grate

    The culvert now has a grate. Its gaps are smaller than a guppy.

  2. Bubble monitoring

    All bubbles are now inspected for content.

  3. A higher edge

    The edge of the pond has been raised by 3 cm.

  4. Dashboard review

    Someone now opens the dashboard every day.
