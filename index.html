// DEFAULTS

Protected out of the box.

Enterprise-grade tackle, preinstalled. No config, no manual, no idea.

3 core agents always swimming

  • lurk_agent Watches your data from a respectful distance. Takes no action. Reports feeling "engaged."
  • bubble_agent Emits a status bubble every 400ms. The bubbles are empty. That's the point of bubbles.
  • circle_agent Swims in circles to keep the school's momentum up. Has never arrived anywhere.

4 library lures, opt-in

  • lure_synergy Appends "hook, line, and synergy" to any output, including errors.
  • lure_dashboard Generates a dashboard of your other dashboards. Refreshes hourly. Nobody opens it.
  • lure_pivot Pivots. Direction unspecified. Momentum guaranteed.
  • lure_deep_end Throws your roadmap into the deep end to see if it swims. See all lures →
